# Suggested reply to the organisers

**Subject:** LuminaX - corrected Round 1 addendum for reconsideration

Dear Vasudha,

Thank you for offering to review a corrected addendum. We repeated the analysis
and addressed each point in your feedback:

- all confidence intervals now resample whole seed groups, keeping the six
  variants of a generated world together with equal variant weighting;
- the history gain remains uncertain under the corrected interval, and we have
  revised our conclusion instead of presenting it as established;
- the two directional targets are now explicitly defined as recorded Yes by
  the response deadline, with separate fit, calibration and untouched holdout
  seed blocks;
- profile-completeness results now include coverage, unserved rate, waiting
  time and completed outcomes, plus paired 50% and 100% masking tests;
- guarded cardinality is evaluated on completed outcomes in isolated
  comparisons; and
- state-order clarification is compared with a graph-aware alternative,
  including the trade-off between MSMI and waiting time.
