# LuminaX pitch artwork

These three original PNG assets were generated for the Round 2 presentation.
They use transparent backgrounds and do not copy the Romeo & Juliet logo or a
specific illustration from the website.

The visual reference was the public Romeo & Juliet website, especially its
letter format, literary typography, restrained colours and private
correspondence theme: <https://www.romeojuliet.love/>.

## `paired-cameos.png`

Used on slide 1.

Prompt:

> Two original classical cameo profile silhouettes facing one another,
> connected by one fine looping thread that suggests a considered
> introduction. Minimal editorial engraving with a romantic literary tone.
> Deep plum, muted olive and antique-gold accents. Wide composition. Genuine
> transparent background. No text, logo, watermark, border, hearts or
> photorealism.

## `botanical-engraving.png`

Used on slide 10.

Prompt:

> An original botanical engraving of one climbing rose stem intertwined with
> a delicate jasmine stem. Refined hand-inked editorial engraving with an old
> correspondence aesthetic. Deep plum ink with restrained antique-gold
> accents. Tall composition. Genuine transparent background. No text, logo,
> watermark, border, paper rectangle or drop shadow.

## `letter-seal.png`

Used on backup slide 13.

Prompt:

> An original open handwritten letter with a small broken wax seal and one
> loose ribbon. Elegant hand-drawn engraving with a subtle watercolour wash.
> Warm parchment, deep plum and muted antique gold. Genuine transparent
> background. Blank letter with no readable writing. No logo, watermark,
> background rectangle or cast shadow.
